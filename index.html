Common questions

Before you order.

How do I know whether I need direct thermal or thermal transfer?

Lifespan decides it. If the label is scanned and discarded within months — shipping, despatch, temporary tracking — direct thermal is cheaper and needs no ribbon. If it has to stay legible for a year or more, or survive heat, light or handling, use thermal transfer.

Will your rolls run on my existing printer?

Send the printer make and model with your enquiry. Elysian Labels will review compatibility before confirming the quote.

Can I get a sample before ordering volume?

Ask for sample options in the quote request. Elysian Labels will confirm what is available for the label and application.

What are your minimum order quantities?

Include the required quantity or monthly usage in your request. Elysian Labels will confirm the applicable minimum and pricing.

Do you deliver outside your immediate area?

Include the delivery area in your request. Elysian Labels will confirm availability, cost and timing with the quote.

Can labels be printed with our branding?

Yes. We offer custom-converted rolls printed with your logo, colours and layout, alongside standard blank stock. Let us know what you need when you request a quote.

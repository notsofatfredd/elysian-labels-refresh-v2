Choosing a printer

Four questions
pick the printer.

Answer these and you're most of the way to the right model. Not sure? Send us the details and we'll recommend one.

Q / 01

Where will it work?

The application and daily volume decide the class of printer.

  • Desktop: counters, offices, clinics, light despatch
  • Industrial: warehouses and production lines
  • Mobile: shelf-edge, picking, deliveries
  • Card: staff ID, access and membership

Q / 02

Direct thermal or thermal transfer?

How long the label must last decides the print method.

  • Direct thermal: no ribbon, short-term labels like shipping
  • Thermal transfer: uses a ribbon, for durable long-life labels
  • Some printers are direct thermal only

Q / 03

How wide is the label?

Every printer has a maximum print width.

  • 2-inch: up to about 54–56 mm
  • 4-inch: up to about 104–108 mm
  • 6-inch: up to 168 mm, for wide pallet labels

Q / 04

How fine is the print?

Resolution, in dots per inch, sets how small text and barcodes can go.

  • 203 dpi: standard barcodes and shipping labels
  • 300 dpi: small text, logos and compact barcodes
  • 600 dpi: very small component and cosmetic labels
